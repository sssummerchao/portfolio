new filters and data visualization

Analyze the market and visualize trends better

Setting a new industry standard

Ensure the workflow is consistent to guide buyers, sellers, and admins through each step, including handling of edge cases. The MVP established a scalable foundation for hourly energy trading and set a new industry standard.

design system

Bridging design and engineering through Storybook

LevelTen's design system existed only in Figma and lacked clear documentation, making it difficult for engineers to implement consistently and for designers to scale. I created a true single source of truth by building a coded component library in Storybook.

Scaling through accessibility and systemized design

I also upgraded accessibility to WCAG standards, and documented responsive behaviors, edge cases, and interactions.

workflow redesign

Streamlining the asset auction workflow

The existing asset auction process was manual, opaque, and heavily dependent on LevelTen admins to coordinate communication and data entry. I redesigned it to digitize and automate workflows, centralizing data in tables to improve transparency.

Enabling participant collaboration

I also enable direct communication between participants which reduce admin workload and friction across the auction lifecycle.
