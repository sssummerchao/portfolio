Research

Who controls what we see

Software systems are built for convenience, but they often shape what users see based on platform priorities rather than user intent. Google’s search algorithms quietly control visibility by favoring large businesses and suppressing certain sites through opaque decisions.

First prototype

My original idea was to design an experience that changes Google's search results without the audience's knowledge. People liked the idea, but they didn't feel any difference if they didn't know the extension was active.

They would like to be notified when their search results are being reorganized. Otherwise, they don't see the need for another algorithm to interfere with their search results.

What if we could "break" the algorithm and set the page free?

Inspired by the bouncing DVD logo, I applied unpredictable motion to search results allowed the experience to feel more dynamic and alive.
